# The Major ArcanAI

*A 40-card oracle deck of sarcastic skeletons — the cards life actually deals you.*

## How to read

Shuffle. Draw one card for a daily nudge, three for past / present / future,
or five when things have truly gone sideways. The deck does not predict the
future; it tells you what you already know, but louder.
