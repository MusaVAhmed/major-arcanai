# The Major ArcanAI

*A 40-card oracle deck of sarcastic skeletons — the cards life actually deals you.*

## How to read

Shuffle. Draw one card for a daily nudge, three for past / present / future,
or five when things have truly gone sideways. The deck does not predict the
future; it tells you what you already know, but louder.

## The cards

**0 — The Zero of Fucks**  
Let go of expectations and pressure. Step into freedom and a fresh start.  
*Reversed:* You claim you don't care. You absolutely do. Admit it.

**I — The Burned Bridge**  
Not everything is meant to be saved. Some endings are necessary.  
*Reversed:* You lit this one too soon. Some bridges deserve an apology and a rebuild.

**II — The Two of Deez**  
Stand confidently on your own. You don't need validation.  
*Reversed:* Fishing for validation again. Deez are not for external approval.

**III — The Lovers**  
Deep connection — romantic, platonic, or with yourself.  
*Reversed:* Disconnection. You're kissing distance from someone you stopped seeing.

**IV — The Iced Coffee**  
Wake up. Face reality. Do what needs to be done.  
*Reversed:* Decaf energy. You're running on fumes and calling it fine.

**V — Death**  
Transformation through endings. Let the old version of you go.  
*Reversed:* Clinging to the old you. Rigor mortis is not a personality.

**VI — The Moon**  
Things are unclear. Trust your intuition and wait.  
*Reversed:* The fog is self-made. Stop mistaking anxiety for intuition.

**VII — Strength**  
Inner calm and patience will carry you through.  
*Reversed:* Forcing it. The lion calms down when you stop wrestling.

**VIII — Perseverance**  
Keep going. You're closer than you think.  
*Reversed:* Stubbornness isn't progress. Maybe it's the wrong staircase.

**IX — The Tea**  
Truth is being revealed. Stay grounded.  
*Reversed:* The gossip turned toxic. Spill less, sip more.

**X — The Last Straw**  
You've reached your limit. Change is inevitable.  
*Reversed:* You keep declaring the last straw, then buying more straws.

**XI — The Audacity**  
Be bold. Take the risk.  
*Reversed:* Bold in the wrong direction. Audacity without follow-through is just noise.

**XII — The Breast Pump**  
You're overgiving. Rest and refill yourself.  
*Reversed:* Running on empty and still giving. Refill the well first.

**XIII — The Bottle**  
Unexpressed emotions will surface eventually.  
*Reversed:* It already burst. Clean up honestly instead of re-corking.

**XIV — The Taco**  
Find joy in small pleasures.  
*Reversed:* Comfort became avoidance. The taco cannot fix this one.

**XV — The Gold Shoulder**  
Beware of ego and image obsession.  
*Reversed:* The mirror is winning. Look outward for once.

**XVI — The Child**  
Nurture your inner child.  
*Reversed:* Your inner child is running the meeting. Adult supervision required.

**XVII — The Mother**  
You also deserve care, not just others.  
*Reversed:* Martyr mode. Care that costs you everything isn't care.

**XVIII — The Mom Boss**  
You're doing too much. Let go of control.  
*Reversed:* Delegation refused, burnout accepted. Put three of the six arms down.

**XIX — The Hermit**  
Withdraw and reflect.  
*Reversed:* Solitude curdled into hiding. Crack the door open.

**XX — The Gardener**  
Growth takes time. Keep nurturing.  
*Reversed:* Overwatering. Some things only grow when you leave them alone.

**XXI — The Cheese Fries**  
You deserve enjoyment.  
*Reversed:* All reward, no task. Earn the fries.

**XXII — The Slice**  
Release toxic attachments.  
*Reversed:* You cut the wrong thread — or keep re-tying the one you cut.

**XXIII — The Empress**  
You are creative and abundant.  
*Reversed:* Creative block by comparison. Make ugly things on purpose.

**XXIV — The Chicken Nugget**  
Comfort and simplicity heal.  
*Reversed:* Comfort became the whole menu. Vary the diet.

**XXV — The Sun**  
Joy and truth are coming to light.  
*Reversed:* Forced positivity. It's okay to sit in the shade sometimes.

**XXVI — The High Priestess**  
Trust your inner wisdom.  
*Reversed:* You already know. You've known for months. Stop asking for signs.

**XXVII — The Zoom Meeting**  
Disconnect from the fake, reconnect with real life.  
*Reversed:* This could have been an email. So could your excuses.

**XXVIII — The Influencer**  
Be authentic — image isn't everything.  
*Reversed:* Performing a life instead of living one. Log off.

**XXIX — The Life of the Party**  
Outward shine can hide inner loneliness.  
*Reversed:* The mask slipped. Let someone see the tear.

**XXX — The Last Hurrah**  
A final celebration before an ending.  
*Reversed:* The party's over and you're still holding the glass. Go home.

**XXXI — The Dead End Job**  
Don't stay stuck in something meaningless.  
*Reversed:* The exit exists. You've just memorized the wallpaper.

**XXXII — The Fiddle-Leaf Fig**  
Balance appearance with reality.  
*Reversed:* All price tag, no roots. Get real or get repotted.

**XXXIII — The Star**  
Hope and healing are ahead.  
*Reversed:* Hope deferred. Healing isn't linear — pour anyway.

**XXXIV — The Devil**  
Break free from unhealthy cycles.  
*Reversed:* Back in the cuffs, voluntarily. You know the way out; you've walked it before.

**XXXV — The Fair Weather Friend**  
Reevaluate shallow relationships.  
*Reversed:* Plot twist: you might be the one holding the umbrella. Check.

**XXXVI — The Tower**  
Breakdown leads to breakthrough.  
*Reversed:* Postponing the collapse. Controlled demolition beats waiting for lightning.

**XXXVII — The Drama Queen**  
Not everything is a crisis. Check the story you're telling yourself.  
*Reversed:* Suppressed feelings staging a coup. A little drama is honest.

**XXXVIII — The Last Flying Fuck**  
Watch it go. Some things no longer deserve your energy.  
*Reversed:* You let it go too fast. Some things deserved your care.

**XXXIX — The Nap**  
Rest is productive. Lie down.  
*Reversed:* Rest became hiding. Get up, hydrate, face one thing.
